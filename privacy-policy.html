Privacy Policy — Ministry of Illumination

Last updated: 2 September 2026. Applies to the Android and iOS application com.harrowgate.illumination.

Short version. Ministry of Illumination has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your puzzle progress is stored only on your device. The app shows optional rewarded advertisements and sells an optional one-off or subscription purchase, and those two features involve Google and RevenueCat. Nothing else leaves your phone.

Who is responsible

Shahriyar Haris Rimon ("we"). Contact: shahriyar048@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

Which tickets you have resolved, which lost property you have recovered, your requisition balance, and your sound and accessibility settings. This is held in your device's own app storage. We cannot see it. If your device has Android Auto Backup or iCloud Backup enabled, your operating system may include it in your personal backup — that is between you and your device maker.

What is shared with third parties

WhoWhyWhat
Google AdMob To show a rewarded advertisement when you choose to watch one for an extra fitting. Advertisements are never required to progress. Your device's advertising identifier, coarse device and app information, and standard ad-serving data. How Google uses this data.
Google Play Billing To process a purchase, if you make one. Handled entirely by Google. We never see or store your payment details. Google Privacy Policy.
RevenueCat To record that a purchase exists so it can be restored on a new device. An anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt. No name, no email, no payment details. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

We do not use analytics, crash reporting, social sign-in, or any advertising network other than Google AdMob. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are used in United States state privacy laws.

Your choices

  • Advertisements are optional. They appear only as a "Departmental Notice" you choose to watch. Declining costs you nothing you were entitled to.
  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any personalised advertisement is requested.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or turn off app tracking on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app. That removes all on-device progress. To have purchase records removed, contact us at the address above.

Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, note it in the app's release notes.
